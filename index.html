At Ruang Titip I started with a 30-person survey and ten interviews, wrote the pricing and a five-year projection, specified the MVP, and led a three-person team into a funded incubation program. Today the site is live and a storage hub is secured.

Eduvia pulled me the other way. Designing for blind, deaf, motor-impaired and dyslexic learners forced me to listen before drawing a single screen, and that habit won first place nationally.

Between those, I wrote business plans on textile waste and coffee-husk biochar, and handled event budgets above Rp300 million. Information Systems gives me the process and data view underneath, so what I design can be built and measured. I know that view well enough to teach it: in 2026 I ran the Business Process Modeling lab for 42 students, from the Bizagi basics to the final exam I wrote myself.
