§ 01 / About

Two spreadsheets, one screen

I'm a fifth-semester Information Systems student at Universitas Brawijaya, and most of what I actually know about business and design, I learned outside a lecture hall — turning a storage-rental idea into a funded startup, and designing a learning app for people with disabilities that ended up winning a national competition.

I like sitting at the point where a business case and an interface meet: the numbers have to work, and so does the screen. Outside RuangTitip and Eduvia, I've managed budgets north of IDR 400 million for student events, taught business process modeling to my juniors, and sat on my faculty's student senate — most of it in service of making sure a plan survives contact with reality, not just the pitch deck.
